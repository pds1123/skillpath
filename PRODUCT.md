# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People building foundational Azure and AWS skills. They open SkillPath to continue a structured learning path, understand a concept, and practise it without first navigating an exam-question catalogue.

## Product Purpose

SkillPath turns cloud-fundamentals material and practice questions into a guided learning experience. Success means the learner always knows what to learn next, can see meaningful curriculum progress, and treats certification preparation as an optional follow-on workflow.

## Brand Personality

Focused, encouraging, and credible. The product should feel calm enough for sustained study while retaining the confident blue-violet identity of the SkillPath logo.

## Anti-references

- Question-bank dashboards dominated by raw item counts and tag-derived categories.
- Exam-prep products that make mocks, readiness scores, and certification codes the primary experience.
- Generic AI gradients, decorative analytics, and dense spreadsheet-like module lists.

## Design Principles

- Put the learner's next action first.
- Organise content through a designed curriculum, then map practice material into it.
- Show progress in lessons, knowledge checks, and modules rather than raw question inventory.
- Keep practice useful but subordinate to learning.
- Keep certification preparation available but visually secondary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve visible keyboard focus, never communicate state through colour alone, support reduced-motion preferences, and keep layouts usable from small phones through desktop screens.
